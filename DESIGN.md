# Super Graphic design

The design presents a signage business as a maker of visible, physical brand identities. It uses original work and direct language.

- Primary magenta: #7B1B4C; charcoal: #121A29; soft pink highlight: #EEA7CC; white and neutral pale gray surfaces.
- Manrope typography, consistent with the source website.
- Image-led, full-width hero with three distinct project narratives and manual controls.
- Fixed slide geometry with opacity-only transitions; 590px phone height.
- Service selector gives eight services equal prominence.
- Large asymmetric homepage project layout; filterable masonry gallery.
- Magenta company section and a call-first closing section.
- Keyboard-friendly service tabs, native modal image viewer, visible focus and reduced-motion support.
